Diversity & Inclusion

The workshop welcomes contributions from researchers across computer-science groups, humanities departments, archaeological missions, museums and independent research teams worldwide. Our programme committee spans Europe, East Asia, North America and the Middle East, combining computational linguists and domain philologists. We encourage submissions from early-career researchers, graduate students, and scholars from communities traditionally under-represented in ACL-affiliated events. Shared-task participation is open to unaffiliated teams, and shared-task datasets are released under open licences.
